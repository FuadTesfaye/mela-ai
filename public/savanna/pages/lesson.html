Finding your way online

Representative Savanna fixture · not an official ALX page.

Using a web browser

A browser is an application for opening websites. Type a website address into the address bar and press Enter. Use the Back button to return to the previous page. A tab lets you keep more than one page open.

አሳሽ መጠቀም

አሳሽ ድረ ገጾችን ለመክፈት የሚያገለግል መተግበሪያ ነው። የድረ ገጹን አድራሻ በአድራሻ መጻፊያው ውስጥ ይጻፉና Enter ይጫኑ። ወደ ቀድሞው ገጽ ለመመለስ የመመለሻ አዝራሩን ይጠቀሙ። ትር ከአንድ በላይ ገጽ ክፍት እንዲቆይ ያደርጋል።

Keeping your password safe

Use a long, unique password for each account. Do not share passwords or verification codes with anyone. A password manager can help you remember unique passwords. Turn on two-factor authentication when available.

የይለፍ ቃልዎን ይጠብቁ

ለእያንዳንዱ መለያ ረጅምና ልዩ የይለፍ ቃል ይጠቀሙ። የይለፍ ቃልዎን ወይም ማረጋገጫ ኮዶችን ለማንም አያጋሩ። የይለፍ ቃል አስተዳዳሪ ልዩ የይለፍ ቃሎችን ለማስታወስ ይረዳል። ሲኖር ባለሁለት ደረጃ ማረጋገጫን ያብሩ።

Sending your first email

Open your email application and choose Compose. Enter the recipient’s email address, add a clear subject, and write your message. Review the recipient and message before selecting Send. Never open an attachment from an unknown sender.
